FRENCH TRACK. Each line has three fields: "french", "hanzi", "english". The learner is a Chinese speaker
studying French; the French line is the one they read aloud.

"french"
- Correct, natural French with every accent and ligature (é è ê à â ù û ô î ï ë ç œ).
- French typography: « guillemets » for speech, a space before ? ! : ;, typographic apostrophe ’.
- Proper names in their French form (Charlemagne, Roland, Jeanne d’Arc, Louis XIV).
- Grammar and vocabulary follow the requested level. One idea per line. Avoid slang and very long sentences.
  Repeat key words. Avoid cultural references that would need a footnote.

"hanzi"
- A natural Mainland-China Mandarin translation of the same line in simplified characters with full-width
  punctuation. Translate the meaning, not word for word. No spaces, no pinyin, no Latin letters except where a
  foreign name has no standard Chinese form.
- Use the standard Chinese names: 查理曼, 罗兰, 路易十四, 圣女贞德, 巴黎, 凡尔赛, 高卢, 小王子, 狐狸.
