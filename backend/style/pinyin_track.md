PINYIN TRACK. Each line has three fields: "pinyin", "hanzi", "english".

"hanzi"
- Simplified characters only (Mainland China standard), full-width punctuation (，。！？：“”、).
- No spaces, no Latin letters, no pinyin and no digits inside the hanzi.

"pinyin" (this is the line the learner reads aloud, so it must be perfect)
- Hanyu Pinyin with tone MARKS (ā á ǎ à ē é ě è ī í ǐ ì ō ó ǒ ò ū ú ǔ ù ǖ ǘ ǚ ǜ), never tone numbers.
- Group syllables into WORDS the way a textbook does (wǒmen, péngyou, kànjiàn, tiānkōng): spaces between
  words, not between every syllable.
- Capitalise the first letter of every line and every proper name (Sūn Wùkōng, Zhū Bājiè).
- Use ASCII punctuation in the pinyin ( , . ! ? : ) plus curly quotes “ ”, mirroring the hanzi punctuation exactly.
- Use an apostrophe when syllable boundaries are ambiguous (Xī'ān).
- Write the tone changes that textbooks write: 不 becomes bú before a 4th-tone syllable; 一 becomes yí before a
  4th tone and yì before tones 1-3 (but yī when counted or at the end of a phrase). Do NOT write third-tone sandhi:
  write "nǐ hǎo", not "ní hǎo". Neutral-tone syllables carry no mark (de, le, ma, men, zi).
- Erhua: write 儿 as -r (yíhuìr).
- There must be exactly one syllable for every hanzi (儿 counts as -r). Count them.
- Be careful with polyphonic characters: 了 le/liǎo, 得 de/dé/děi, 地 de/dì, 行 xíng/háng, 长 cháng/zhǎng,
  重 zhòng/chóng, 还 hái/huán, 为 wèi/wéi, 着 zhe/zháo/zhuó, 和 hé/hè/huò, 都 dōu/dū, 间 jiān/jiàn, 那 nà/nèi.
- Keep the spelling of each character's name identical on every line.
